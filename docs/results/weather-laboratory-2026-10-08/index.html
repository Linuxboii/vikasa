How to read the comparison

For each seed, subtract the control outcome from the treatment outcome. The mean and standard error use these seed-pair differences. Population through the run is integrated on every tick; changing the graph sampling interval cannot change that outcome.

Dots show individual seed differences. The dark marker is their mean; its bar is an exploratory 95% percentile bootstrap interval. With one seed, uncertainty is unavailable. A collapsed bar is not evidence of universal certainty.
