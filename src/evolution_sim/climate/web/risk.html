What this is based on

    Climate forcing is observed data (NASA POWER MERRA-2, 1981–2025) and IPCC AR6 WG1 Table 4.5 warming medians. The organism is Vikasa's generic individual-based model: inherited thermal optimum, asymmetric thermal performance, heat-induced sterility. Species presets are illustrative; use a species' own generation time and tolerance for a specific assessment. Open the full evidence report.
